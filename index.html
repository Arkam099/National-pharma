Who We Are

Lahore's Most Reliable Pharma Distribution Partner

National Pharma is a leading B2B pharmaceutical distributor headquartered at Kotlakhpat, Lahore. We operate exclusively in the wholesale supply chain — our medicines go directly to registered pharmacies, hospitals, dispensaries, and clinics, not individual consumers.

With a robust cold chain infrastructure, verified sourcing from DRAP-approved manufacturers, and a dedicated logistics fleet, we ensure every batch arrives authenticated, in-spec, and on time.
